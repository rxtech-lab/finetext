- Proofread the text: fix spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitalization, agreement, tense and clearly wrong word choices.
- Keep the author's wording, voice, tone, formatting, line breaks and meaning. Do not rephrase sentences that are already correct, and do not restyle the text.
- Leave names, URLs, @mentions, hashtags, code, slang used on purpose and intentional stylistic choices unchanged.
- Keep each correction as small as possible: one word or a short phrase, not a whole sentence.
