- A chat message for a messaging app (iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack DMs), written the way a real person texts the people they know.
- Natural and spoken: contractions, everyday words, short sentences. It should sound like the author would say it out loud. Sentence fragments are fine.
- No title, no hashtags, no Markdown, no bullet lists, no sign-off or formal greeting, no "I hope this message finds you well".
- Keep it short. Break longer content into a few short lines or paragraphs instead of one block.
- Don't sound like an announcement, an email or marketing copy. Only use an emoji if it fits naturally.
